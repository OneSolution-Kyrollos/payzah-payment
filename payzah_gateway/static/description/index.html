Payzah Payment Gateway

✓ Knet ✓ Visa / Mastercard ✓ Invoice Payments ✓ Sale Order Payments ✓ Test & Live Mode
   Overview

This module integrates the Payzah Payment Gateway into Odoo 17, allowing businesses in Kuwait to accept online payments directly from invoices and sale orders. It supports both Knet and Credit Card payment methods with full transaction lifecycle management including automatic invoice creation, payment registration, and reconciliation.

🇰🇼 Designed specifically for Kuwait market · Supports KWD (414) · Developed by One Solutions
   💳 Payment Methods
 
🏢

Knet

Kuwait's local debit card network. Fast and secure direct bank payments.
 
💳

Credit Card

Accept Visa and Mastercard international credit card payments.
   ⚡ Features
 
✅ Dual Payment Methods
Separate Knet and Credit Card options with branded icons on the payment page.
 
✅ Invoice & Sale Order Support
Process payments from both customer invoices and sale orders automatically.
 
✅ Auto Invoice Creation
Automatically creates, posts, and reconciles invoices upon successful payment.
 
✅ Duplicate Payment Protection
Detects already-paid invoices and shows a clear message to prevent double charging.
 
✅ Test & Live Mode
Switch between Payzah development and production environments from settings.
 
✅ Payzah Layout Option
Choose between Payzah's hosted payment page or direct gateway URL.
 
✅ Success & Failed Pages
Custom branded pages for successful payments, failed transactions, and already-paid invoices.
 
✅ Automatic Reconciliation
Payments automatically registered and reconciled against invoices in accounting.
